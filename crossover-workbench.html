Acoustic response

Each colored line is one way: a driver heard through its crossover parts. The black sum is what you hear, the drivers added together with their phase. Where two ways overlap, they only add up to a flat sum if their levels and phases line up.

Parts & power

W into 8 Ω

Pink noise spreads the test power evenly per octave from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, close to music. Worst sine is the highest dissipation from a single tone anywhere in that range. Pick resistor ratings above the pink figure with margin.

Feature set inspired by Jeff Bagby's Passive Crossover Designer (2001–2019), a gift to the DIY speaker community. This is an independent implementation and uses no code or data from it.

Model limits: ideal parts except inductor DCR, one flat rectangular baffle, piston directivity, and a simplified edge-diffraction model with a dipole option for open baffles. Spinorama curves are estimates built from the front hemisphere. Imported measurements with phase from a common mic position give the most trustworthy sums.
